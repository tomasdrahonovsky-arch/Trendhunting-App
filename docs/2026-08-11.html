Third, the body as contested territory continues to intensify. For non-alcoholic beverage strategists, the implications are significant: drinks must now navigate a landscape where shared consumption is no longer guaranteed, where heritage and provenance carry emotional weight, and where functional claims must compete with pharmaceutical interventions. The opportunity lies in creating products that bridge these fragmenting rituals—beverages that can accommodate divergent dietary protocols while still enabling connection.
